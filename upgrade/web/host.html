Gesture cheat sheet

  • 1 fist hold 3s → lights ON
  • Open palm hold 3s → lights OFF
  • 1 / 2 / 3 fingers hold 2s → fan speed 1 / 2 / 3
  • Thumbs up hold 2s → fan STOP
  • 2 closed fists hold 2s → door toggle
  • Wave or 2 open palms hold 2s → security ON/OFF
