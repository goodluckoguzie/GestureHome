What is IoT?

Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects (things) fitted with sensors, software, and connectivity so they can collect data, communicate, and act, often with little or no human input.

AWS: IoT is the network of connected devices plus the tech that lets them talk to each other and to the cloud. Coursera: devices exchange data without a person in the loop; Kevin Ashton, 1999. IoT Portal: sensors or actuators on a network; closed loop sense, decide, act. USB is still connectivity.

The problem

Smart homes still ask us to touch shared surfaces, switches, phones, and keypads.

IoT can act, but control still means touch. Shared gym grips and keypads. COVID: wash hands with soap. Also busy hands and PIN pads. Do not preview gestures yet.

The idea: GestureHome

One touch-free gesture language controls the whole home.

Touch-free answer to the problem slide. Keep this conceptual: one language, many devices. Do not explain every gesture here. The map comes next.
